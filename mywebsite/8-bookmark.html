Hunza Valley, Gilgit-Baltistan

Famous for dramatic mountain scenery, turquoise lakes, terraced villages, and views of peaks such as Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar. Popular places nearby include Attabad Lake, Passu, and Khunjerab Pass.
